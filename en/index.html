How I work

Four stages, with the system running from the first weeks so you can try it with your own data.

  1. Understand

    You tell me how your team works today and what you want to solve. I write down the business rules before writing a single line of code.

  2. Build with you watching

    Every step is published for you to try. Whatever doesn’t work gets changed right away.

  3. Deliver

    Initial data load, training for each role and a plain-language manual.

  4. Maintain

    Updates, backups and adjustments for a monthly fee agreed in advance.

Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask before getting started.

How much does a system cost?

It depends on what you need, so there are no list prices. After a first conversation I send you a free quote, with a fixed price for development and a fixed monthly fee for maintenance. You know in advance what you will pay. For companies outside Uruguay, prices are always in US dollars.

How long does it take?

Work is done in stages, and the first part is up and running within a few weeks so you can try it with your data. A full system is built piece by piece, without waiting months to see results. The timeline for each stage is written in the quote.

Who owns the data and the system?

The data always belongs to your company and you can export all of it at any time. You use the system under a license while the service is active; that way I can maintain it, improve it and stand behind it.

What if I want to cancel the service?

Just give 30 days’ notice. During that time you take all your data in files you can open with Excel or other programs, so you are never locked in.

What if something breaks?

You write to me and I handle it by severity: if the system can’t be used, I respond within hours; if it’s an annoyance but you can keep working, within the business day; and questions within three business days. Everything is also backed up daily.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use it?

No. Each system comes with training for each person based on their role and a plain-language manual. And if anyone gets stuck, they can write to me.

Do you work with companies outside Uruguay?

Yes. I work remotely, with video-call meetings. For companies abroad, prices are always in US dollars.

Got something to sort out?

Tell me in a couple of lines what your company does and what you’d like to solve. I reply the same day.
